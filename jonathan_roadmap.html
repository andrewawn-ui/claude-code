Phase 1 — Foundation

⚡ Week 1 · Day 1–7
Week 1 — Map Your Eating Windows
Right now you eat at 2pm, 6pm, and 11pm. The issue is timing and spacing — not willpower. This week you map out exactly when and what those windows look like.
This week
  • Write down your current eating windows: 2pm, 6pm, 11pm — and what you actually eat at each
  • Take Day 1 bodyweight (currently 150lbs) and a progress photo
  • List your 5 most common takeout spots — near the store and at home
  • Set reminders for each eating window: 2pm, 6pm, 8pm pre-gym, post-gym
  • Identify which meal is currently your lowest calorie meal and why
🧠 Mind 🗺️ Identity
🔥 Week 2 · Day 8–14
Week 2 — Build the Nutrition System
You eat takeout most days. That's fine for now. This week you build a curated list of what to order at each spot so the decision is already made before you're hungry.
This week
  • For each regular spot (Lazeez, Big Way, pho etc.) write down the highest-protein order
  • Reduce Lazeez to 1x this week — replace the other 2 with a higher-protein option
  • Identify one meal you can have ready before heading to the store at 2pm
  • Log what you eat today — just once, to see the actual calorie picture
  • Set the rule: if you don't know what to eat, default to the list you just made
🍖 Fuel 📋 System
💪 Week 3 · Day 15–21
Week 3 — Training Log + Step Count
You already train 4–6x/week with good form. The gap is tracking. This week you start logging sessions and step count so progress is visible in numbers.
This week
  • Log every gym session this week: exercises, sets, reps, weights used
  • Track step count daily — you're walking the store 2–8pm, get a baseline
  • After each session (9–11pm), eat something with 40g+ protein before sleeping
  • Note your energy level at the start of each workout — 1 to 10
  • Identify the one lift where you've been plateauing the longest
💪 Body 📋 Tracking
🏆 Week 4 · Day 22–30
Week 4 — Month 1 Check-In
Four weeks of real data. Look at weight, eating windows, logged sessions. What's working and what isn't. Adjust the system — don't abandon it.
This week
  • Weigh yourself — compare to Day 1. Target: scale should be moving up toward 160
  • Review your eating window log: which meals were you most consistent on?
  • Review your gym log: are the weights going up week over week?
  • Identify the biggest gap in the system so far — what broke down and why
  • Coach check-in — go through the data together
🧠 Mind 💪 Body 🍖 Fuel

Phase 02 · Days 31–60
Phase 2 — Build
"The system is set. Now you build consistency on top of it."
Weeks 5–8
30d
⚡ Week 5 · Day 31–37
Week 5 — Add the Third Meal
The third eating window needs to be consistent. Week 5 adds a structured post-gym meal timed so it doesn't compete with the 11pm window.
This week
  • Add a pre-gym meal at 8pm (before training at 9pm): 40g protein + carbs
  • New window sequence: 2pm → 6pm → 8pm pre-gym → post-gym shake
  • Test this for the full week — note if gym performance changes
  • Stop eating Lazeez after the gym at 11pm — replace with a faster option
  • Weigh yourself mid-week — the scale should start responding
💪 Body ⚔️ Progression
🔥 Week 6 · Day 38–44
Week 6 — Set Calorie + Protein Targets
You know what you eat. Now you know how much. Protein target and calorie target get set this week — based on your bodyweight and goal. No need to track every meal.
This week
  • Calculate your protein target: 150lbs × 1g = 150g protein per day minimum
  • Calculate your calorie target for gaining: 150 × 17 = ~2,550 calories per day
  • Map your current meals to these numbers — are you hitting 2,550 calories?
  • If not: identify which meal is easiest to add 300–400 calories to
  • You don't need to track every meal — just know if you're above or below target
🍖 Fuel 📊 Data
💪 Week 7 · Day 45–51
Week 7 — Remove Decision Fatigue
You mentioned decision fatigue — too many options, not knowing what to eat. Week 7 removes the decisions entirely. Pre-set choices for each time of day.
This week
  • List every food decision you make in a day — from waking up to post-gym
  • For each decision point, write a default answer so you never have to think about it
  • Example: 'Before store I eat X. At the store at 6pm, I order X from X.'
  • Send the pre-built takeout list to your coach so he can verify the choices
  • Rule: if the decision is already made, you only have to execute
🧠 Mind 🔥 Discipline
🏆 Week 8 · Day 52–60
Week 8 — 60-Day Review
60 days of data. Weight trend, eating consistency, training output. Side-by-side with Day 1. The progress is in the numbers — this is where it becomes visible.
This week
  • Take Day 60 progress photo — compare side-by-side with Day 1
  • Weigh yourself: target is to be 3–5lbs heavier than Day 1 by now
  • Run your best lifts this week — set personal records on key movements
  • Rate the 3 pillars: Meal Timing / Takeout System / Training Log — each /10
  • Coach check-in — adjust the plan for the final 60 days based on the data
🧠 Mind 💪 Body 🍖 Fuel

Phase 3 — Optimize

⚡ Week 9 · Day 61–67
Week 9 — Progressive Overload Focus
The eating system is running. Now you push the training harder — add one more set per exercise, increase weights where possible. This is when physique change accelerates.
This week
  • Add one more working set to every exercise this week
  • Increase weight by 5–10% on any lift where last week felt easy
  • Keep all eating windows locked — do not skip the 8pm pre-gym meal
  • Weigh yourself 3x this week — confirm the trend is still going up
  • Film one training session to review form and see your physique progress
💪 Body 🔥 Intensity
🔥 Week 10 · Day 68–74
Week 10 — Fine-Tune the System
Fine-tune based on the 60-day data. If weight gain has stalled, calories go up. If energy dips, meal timing shifts. Small adjustments at this stage make a big difference.
This week
  • If weight gain has stalled: add 300 calories to the 2pm meal
  • If energy is low pre-gym: move the 8pm meal to 7:30pm and add carbs
  • Review your takeout list — are you actually using it or still guessing?
  • Protein: confirm you are hitting 150g+ every day this week
  • Hydrate: minimum 3L water daily — this directly affects gym performance
🍖 Fuel ⚔️ Precision
💪 Week 11 · Day 75–81
Week 11 — Make It Automatic
Which habits are now automatic? Which still need effort? This week you identify everything still requiring willpower and build a system to remove that friction.
This week
  • Write down which habits are now automatic (no thought required)
  • Write down which habits still require effort — these need more reinforcement
  • Update your takeout list — any new spots or better orders since Week 2?
  • Confirm you are hitting 150g+ protein daily without tracking every meal
  • Train all scheduled sessions — no skipping in the final phase
🧠 Mind 🗺️ Legacy
⚔️ Week 12 · Day 82–90
Week 12 — 90-Day Review
90 days of data. Compare everything to Day 1 — weight, training, eating consistency. Set exact targets for the final 30 days.
This week
  • Take Day 90 progress photo — compare side-by-side with Day 1 and Day 60
  • Weigh yourself: note your total change from 150lbs
  • Run your Day 90 PR test on all key lifts
  • Score yourself on all 3 pillars vs Day 1 — how much has actually changed?
  • Coach check-in — celebrate wins, set exact targets for the last 30 days

⚡ Week 13 · Day 91–97
Week 13 — Final Push on Training
Month 4 is where your body remembers everything you taught it. Push every session like it is the last one.
This week
  • Add one extra set to every main compound lift this week
  • Confirm bodyweight trend — should be 4–6lbs above Day 1 by now
  • All three eating windows hit every single day this week — no exceptions
  • Log step count daily — minimum 8,000 on rest days
  • Take a progress photo — compare to Week 8 side by side
💪 Body 🔥 Intensity
🔥 Week 14 · Day 98–104
Week 14 — Automate Everything
The system is automatic. This is the week you sharpen the blade one last time.
This week
  • Write down every food and training decision you still make manually
  • For each remaining decision — create a permanent default and write it down
  • Set up the permanent version of your daily schedule: wake, eat, store, gym, sleep
  • What would your routine look like at Day 180 — write it out now
  • Identify one thing you want to add to the routine after Day 120
🍖 Fuel ⚔️ Precision
💪 Week 15 · Day 105–111
Week 15 — Beyond Day 120
Champions don't stop when the program ends. This week you design exactly what the routine looks like going forward — and set the next 90-day target.
This week
  • Write your permanent 'Jonathan eats like this every day' food template
  • Set your next weight target and the date you want to hit it by
  • Run your best lifts this week — set Week 15 personal records
  • Write down what you would tell yourself on Day 1 knowing what you know now
  • Identify one new habit to add to the system after Day 120

Week 16 — Day 120 Assessment
120 days. You built the system. You stayed consistent. You are not the same person who started.
This week

  • Final progress photos — front and side, same conditions as Day 1
  • Run your Day 120 PR test on all key lifts
  • Write down what changed: weight, physique, energy, routine, confidence
